      UK Breast Intergroup Meetings 1st May and 17th November 2020

      Dear members,

      To help you plan your agenda for 2020, we would like to inform you that the upcoming UKBI meetings will be held on the following dates:

      • UKBI, Friday, 1st May 2020 (10.30-4pm) – Lecture Theatre – The Institute of Cancer Research, Chester Beatty Laboratories, 237 Fulham Road, SW3 6JB  

      • UKBI, Tuesday, 17th November 2020 (10.30-4pm) – Lecture Theatre – The Institute of Cancer Research, Chester Beatty Laboratories, 237 Fulham Road, SW3 6JB

      The details and registration forms will be circulated closer to the dates.
