      Within your social media profiles, it is possible to add &#8216;frames&#8217; around your picture, to show your affiliation and support for a particular campaign. For Breast Cancer Awareness month, as METUP UK continues to [fight for more attention to be paid to secondary breast cancer](/aims-and-objectives/), we have created a frame that you can add to your Facebook profile picture, and we would really appreciate your support for this. It looks like this:

      <img src="/media/2020/10/frane-for-metup-uk-300x300.png" alt="METUP UK (Empty) Frame for Facebook" width="300" height="300" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

      ## Adding the Frame

      If you are on your mobile phone, and have seen the frame on someone else&#8217;s profile, it may be as simple as clicking on &#8216;Try Me&#8217; to add the frame to your image:

      <img src="/media/2020/10/2020-10-03-15.09.17-473x1024.png" alt="'Try Me' to add frame on Social Media Profile" width="473" height="1024" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

      On a laptop/desktop, go to your profile picture, and select &#8216;update profile picture&#8217;:

      <img src="/media/2020/10/change-profile.png" alt="Changing Profile Image" width="389" height="451" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

      An option box will appear, offering you some suggested photos, or the option to upload a new picture, which will then post as your profile picture. To add a frame, click on &#8216;Add frame&#8217;, and then choose a frame from the search .. METUP should find ours, once Facebook approves it (it can take a few days for Facebook to show new frames):

      <img src="/media/2020/10/add-frame.png" alt="Add frame" width="812" height="725" loading="lazy" decoding="async"> If for some reason,  neither of these options works, you can click on <a href="https://www.facebook.com/profilepicframes/?selected_overlay_id=795558247873706" rel="noopener" target="_blank">this Facebook link</a>, and it will take you to the frame.

      ## If you have a pre-existing frame

      If you already have a &#8216;frame&#8217; around your existing profile picture, it will try and add the new frame on top of the pre-existing image, so you may need to &#8216;select profile picture or video&#8217;, find an image from photos you have already uploaded, and *then* &#8216;add frame&#8217;:

      <img src="/media/2020/10/2020-10-03-15.33.11-473x1024.png" alt width="473" height="1024" loading="lazy" decoding="async">

      You can choose whether to have the image stay there permanently, or just &#8216;temporary&#8217;, such as for a month (when you could <a href="https://www.dummies.com/social-media/facebook/add-frame-facebook-profile-picture/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">find another frame</a>).

      ## Using the image elsewhere

      Once the frame has been added, the &#8216;new profile picture&#8217; image will appear in your timeline, allowing others to see it (and use it). You could think about &#8216;tagging&#8217; yourself in it, and/or downloading the image (right-click, then &#8216;Save image as&#8217;), which you could then use on other social media profile images.
